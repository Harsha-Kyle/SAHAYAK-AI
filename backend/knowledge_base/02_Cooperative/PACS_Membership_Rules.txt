PACS MEMBERSHIP & GOVERNANCE RULES
Ministry of Cooperation, Government of India

Sec 21. Membership Admission
Any adult farmer residing in the area of operation of a Primary Agricultural Credit Society (PACS) who holds cultivable land or carries out agricultural activities can become a member by purchasing a minimum of 1 share of the society.

Sec 22. Rights of Members
1. Right to receive short-term crop loans at subsidized interest rates.
2. Right to vote in the Annual General Body Meeting (AGM).
3. Right to purchase fertilizers, seeds, and farm equipment at fair prices.
4. Right to receive dividend declared out of net society profits.

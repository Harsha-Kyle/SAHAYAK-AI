PM-KISAN SAMMAN NIDHI OPERATIONAL GUIDELINES
Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture

Sec 1. Scheme Overview
Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) is a Central Sector scheme to provide income support to all landholding farmers' families in the country to enable them to take care of expenses related to agriculture and allied activities as well as domestic needs.

Sec 2. Financial Benefit
Under the Scheme, an amount of Rs. 6000/- per year is transferred in three 4-monthly instalments of Rs. 2000/- directly into the bank accounts of the beneficiary farmers' families.

Sec 4. Eligibility Criteria and Exclusions
All landholding farmers' families, which have cultivable landholding in their names are eligible to get benefit under the scheme.
Exclusions:
1. Institutional land holders.
2. Farmer families in which one or more of its members belong to former and present holders of constitutional posts.
3. Income Tax payers in last assessment year.
4. Mandatory e-KYC is required to release instalments.
